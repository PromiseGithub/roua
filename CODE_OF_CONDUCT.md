# Code of conduct

ROUA is a security project, so we argue about details often. Do it with respect.

- Be welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- No harassment, insults or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Do not publish anyone's private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these expectations. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer, [@PromiseGithub](https://github.com/PromiseGithub), on GitHub.
